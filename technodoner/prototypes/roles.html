One screen

Bärbel (left screen) uses ←→ to pick a customer, Enter to serve or start a dance, U I O J K L to sign ingredients and N M , . for words. Bruno (right screen) uses Q for pita, W for the spit and E R T A S for the bins and sauces. He chops with D, passes with F, trashes with X and answers with 1–4. You can also click everything. Bruno shouldn’t peek at the left screen.

How to play

Bärbel at the window is the only one who sees the customers. She can’t touch food. She reads each order, signs the ingredients to Bruno, and serves whatever lands on the pass to the right customer.

Bruno at the grill builds every döner but can’t see the window or hear a thing. He takes a pita, shaves meat off the spit, adds veg from the bins and sauce from the bottles, then slides it onto the pass. Empty bins need chopping first.

Recipes (only Bärbel can see which one was ordered)

Paw signs

1 Fleisch · 2 Salat · 3 Tomate · 4 Zwiebel · 5 Knoblauch · 6 Scharf, plus JA, NEIN, ? and LOS!. Bruno can answer with JA, NEIN, ? and LOS!.

The shifts

Kotti Imbiss: learn the roles. Bunker Club: orders arrive as dances. Bärbel copies the moves to decode each ticket. U8 Nachtzug: Bruno’s kitchen goes pitch black in tunnels and every stop shuffles his bins. Bärbel lights a bin with her torch, and can only serve while the doors are open.

Controls on your own device

BärbelTap a customer, or use ←→ + Enter. Arrow keys or the pad to dance. Tap a sign, or use U I O J K L / N M , .. ⌫ sends a döner back. Torch: tap a bin or 6–0.
BrunoTap stations, or use Q pita, W spit, E R T A S bins, D chop, F pass, X trash, 1–4 reply.
