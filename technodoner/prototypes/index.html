How to play

Build each döner on a pita, then hand it out at the window. Bread from the crate, meat shaved off the spit, veg chopped on a board, sauce from the bottles. Customers who wait too long leave, and that costs you €5.

Bread crate: grab Spit: hold CHOP (into a döner or empty paws) Veg: put on a board, hold CHOP Sauce: grab with a döner in paw
