Döner = bread (B crate) + shaved meat (hold chop at the spit) + chopped veg (chop on a board) + sauce (walk to a bottle). Serve it at the window. In the Bunker, customers order by dancing: stand under the window, press chop to start dancing, then copy their moves with your direction keys to decode the ticket.
