🐻 TECHNODÖNER 🥙

Original

A döner stand inside a Berlin techno club. The bass is too loud to talk. The regulars dance their order, and you only have your paws.

🧾

Cashier Bear

You see the regulars. Copy their dance with your body (camera) or keys, then hand over the döner.
🔪

Chef Bear

You have the book of regulars and the ingredients. Read the cashier's dance in your window and cook their usual.

Each player opens their role on their own screen. Don't look at each other's screens!
